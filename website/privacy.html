Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 5, 2026

At CloudStream TV, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how CloudStream TV handles data, permissions, and credentials when you use our Android TV application.

1. Personal Information Collection

CloudStream TV does not collect, harvest, or store any personal data. Our application is designed as a client-side utility. We do not use external analytics packages, and we do not maintain user databases on our servers.

2. Google Drive Permissions & Scopes

To browse and play media files, the app requests the following Google OAuth scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly: This allows the app to read directory structures and obtain media streaming endpoints from folders you choose. It does not allow modifying, deleting, or editing any of your Google Drive contents.

3. OAuth Tokens & Local Storage

Any credentials (access tokens, refresh tokens, folder nicknames, and stream history) are stored locally and securely on your Android TV device inside encrypted private preferences (SharedPreferences).

These credentials never leave your TV device except when making direct requests to official Google Drive API endpoints (`googleapis.com`) to stream media.

4. OAuth Backend Helper

Because standard TV devices lack efficient input methods, the app utilizes a helper backend bridge (`/api/session`, `/api/poll`) to complete the Google OAuth login sequence.

  • The backend holds session cache inside short-lived, transient in-memory structures.
  • No authentication tokens, email addresses, or session codes are stored permanently on the server.
  • Session tokens are deleted from the backend memory instantly once they are successfully fetched by your TV application.

5. Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly track or capture information from children. Our app is safe for family use.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 5, 2026

CloudStream TV ("we", "our", or "the application") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to data protection and outlines how we handle information when you install and use our Android TV application.

1. No Data Collection or Tracking

CloudStream TV is designed with a strict zero-collection policy. We do not gather, store, track, or share any personal data, usage metrics, location information, or search history. There are no tracking scripts, telemetry modules, or analytics SDKs embedded in the application.

2. Google API & Scope Usage

The application interacts directly with the Google Drive REST API to browse files and stream media. When you authenticate via Google Sign-In, the app requests the following read-only permissions:

  • Google Drive Read-Only Access (`drive.readonly`): This permission is used solely to discover, fetch metadata (filenames, sizes, thumbnails), and stream video/photo files inside your linked folders.
  • User Email Address (`email`): Used locally on your TV screen to identify which Google Account is currently active in the sidebar.

The application **does not** delete, write to, or modify any files on your Google Drive.

3. Local Storage & Encryption

To provide a persistent logging experience, all credentials, folders, and preferences are stored exclusively on your local TV device:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally using Android's private `SharedPreferences` storage. They are never transmitted to any third-party servers.
  • All API queries and requests for file structures are sent directly and securely from your TV device to Google's official API servers (`https://www.googleapis.com`) using standard HTTPS encryption.

4. OAuth Web Login Bridge

To facilitate sign-in on limited-input TV devices, the app utilizes a custom OAuth backend bridge. This bridge only acts as a temporary buffer to exchange Google's authentication response:

  • It holds the session token only while the TV is polling.
  • The session data is permanently deleted from the bridge server immediately upon delivery of tokens to the TV app, or automatically deleted after 10 minutes of inactivity.
  • The bridge server does not log or persist any account details or auth tokens in database records.
